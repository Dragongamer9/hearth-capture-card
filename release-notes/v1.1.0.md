Capture Card 1.1: fixes for the Elgato 4K S (and any card that sends nothing in the mode you picked).

- Never freezes: each way to get the picture is tried for 3 seconds, best first (the graphics card's decoder, the
  CPU's, the card's own best frame rate, then YUYV 1080p60), and stopping the card never waits more than 3 seconds.
  Settings and the rest of the window always answer.
- No more restarting the card again and again: reading a card's modes no longer counts as the card changing (on the
  Elgato 4K S it restarted about 25 times in a few seconds and could switch the card off).
- "No signal" is said only after every way was tried.
- Your laptop's own camera is no longer listed as a capture card.
- Every try is written to the terminal, so a card that still won't show a picture says why.
