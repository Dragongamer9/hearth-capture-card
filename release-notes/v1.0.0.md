Capture Card 1.0, the first release.

- Your Xbox (or any HDMI) on this PC's screen through a USB capture card, full screen, with its sound.
- A slim bar that hides by itself: the card, the signal, the delay, volume, Fit / Fill / Stretch, screenshot and record.
- Recordings (MP4, 1080p60) and screenshots, each with its own page.
- "No signal" and "No card" pages that pick the picture up by themselves.
- Game Mode: the viewer full screen with a quick menu for the controller.
- Settings: the card, its mode, the sound and where things are saved.

Install it from the Hearth Store on Hearth OS, or download the AppImage below and run it. Needs testing with a real card.
