Capture Card 1.2: the card's sound, with the picture, on the Elgato 4K S.

- The capture card's sound plays: it's taken and played the way OBS does (through PipeWire's PulseAudio side), so the
  Elgato 4K S's sound comes through instead of nothing.
- Picking the card's sound no longer turns the picture black: the sound runs on its own, apart from the picture, and
  changing a sound setting only restarts the sound.
- Smoother sound: no more choppy, repeating sound on Intel laptops.
- Stopping and starting again never clash.
